Order Methods & Availability

Terra Taste operates primarily on online orders with options for delivery, pick-up, and meet-up.

Because we also actively participate, visit, and host booths at local events and festivals, our schedule and physical locations can sometimes shift. We love to see you there in person!

Advance Ordering & Email Instructions

Since we often receive a high volume of orders during special events, we recommend ordering in advance so we can ensure everything is prepared perfectly and nothing gets missed.

To place your advance order accurately, please copy the template below, fill out your details, and send it directly to our main email address: terrataste@outlook.com.

Copy Order Template:

Name: [Your Full Name] Delivery Address / Meet-up Location: [Your Address or Location] Preferred Items & Quantity: [Item Name & How Many] Preferred Date & Time: [Date & Time] Special Request / Allergies Notes: [Any notes or specific instructions]

Note: You can also use this same email address (terrataste@outlook.com) to reach out for event invitations and general inquiries!
